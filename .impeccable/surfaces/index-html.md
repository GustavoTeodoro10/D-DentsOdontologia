# Surface brief: index.html (one-page site)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a local, often anxious, deciding by trust on a phone. Action: tap "Agendar avaliação" (WhatsApp).
Constraints: Tailwind CDN + vanilla JS, light and dark via prefers-color-scheme, no invented facts, own photos only, GoatCounter events on every CTA.
Direction is user-pinned (approved at the taste-skill checkpoint), so no concept-seed roll was run.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Warmth proven by evidence. The page's one idea is that an anxious visitor can verify this clinic is real and kind: the actual storefront, 391 Google reviews, the clinic's own results. It refuses the dental-landing default (teal and white, stock smiles, three icon cards).
OWN-WORLD: Forest green #1E3A2F bars that hold the light logo, a neutral cool-green ground, gold #C9A84C as the only accent (primary button, stars on dark). Bricolage Grotesque display with Hanken Grotesk text. 16px radius on images and surfaces, pill buttons. Dark mode is a deep green-black with the same gold. Recognizable with no content: green logo bar, gold pill, storefront photo held inside the page margins with 16px rounded corners.
STORY: A visitor sees a real front door and a kind headline, confirms the 5,0 rating, skims two honest groups of treatments, sees real results, reads patients describing fear turning into trust, learns how to book and where to find the clinic, then taps WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Green header bar (logo left, four anchors, gold "Agendar avaliação"). Below, on the neutral ground, a 5/11 text column (H1 "Seu sorriso em boas mãos.", one sentence of subtext, gold CTA plus a quiet "Ver tratamentos" link) and a 6/11 storefront photo that stays inside the container margins, rounded on all corners. Mobile: text first, photo full width below, floating WhatsApp button.
FORM: Asymmetric split hero with a contained photo (changed from edge-bleed at the owner's request) (pinned by the taste-skill checkpoint, position 1 of 1), seed key: pinned-by-user.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
