# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Confirmed by the TeoCode standard (CriadorDeSites.md, MODO PRIMEIRO-SITE): static HTML/CSS/JS with Tailwind CSS via CDN. No build step, backend, CMS or blog. Deploy target: GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages.

## Users
People who live or work around Jardim Zaira, Mauá-SP, looking for a dentist. They arrive on a phone, often anxious (several Google reviews mention fear of the dentist) and decide by trust: reviews, a real place, a clear way to book. Their job: confirm this clinic is real and welcoming, then start a WhatsApp conversation to book an evaluation.

## Product Purpose
First website of D+Dents Odontologia e Estética, Jardim Zaira unit. The clinic has no site of its own (the old subdomain page is used only as a content source). Success is a visitor tapping the WhatsApp CTA. The page is also a preview TeoCode sends to the owner, so it must look finished and professional.

## Positioning
A neighbourhood clinic with unusually strong, verifiable local proof: 5,0 on Google with 391 reviews, a real storefront at Av. Presidente Castelo Branco 1422, and the clinic's own published results. A competitor cannot copy that evidence.

## Operating Context
- Single unit, Mon to Fri 09:00 to 19:00, Sat 09:00 to 15:00, closed Sunday (Google Business Profile, collected 2026-10-08).
- Booking happens only through WhatsApp (11) 98885-1597.
- Part of a wider D+Dents network (other units and accounts exist). This site covers the Jardim Zaira unit only and must not use other units' photos or contact data.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- All CTAs go to WhatsApp 5511988851597 with a pre-filled message per section and a unique `data-gc-event`.
- Analytics: GoatCounter only, `https://teocode.goatcounter.com/count`. No cookies, no banner.
- No prices, discounts, promotions or payment terms are published (dental advertising rules are restrictive; the owner decided: no offer section).
- Services shown come from the old site and the unit's Instagram: clinico geral, limpeza, restauracoes, extracoes, proteses, implantes, clareamento, facetas, harmonizacao facial, botox, preenchimento labial. To be confirmed by the owner.
- Open product facts (do not invent): e-mail, responsible dentist name and CRO, the exact list of services, patient consent for the before/after images.

## Brand Commitments
- Real logo only (assets/source/site-antigo/logo-original.png, light version, needs a dark green ground). Never redraw or restyle it. Note: the logo reads "ODONTOLOGICA É ESTÉTICA" (accent on the conjunction, missing accent on Odontológica); left untouched, reported to the owner.
- Palette from the logo and the storefront: forest green #1E3A2F and gold #C9A84C.
- Images: only the clinic's own (storefront photo from Google Maps, before/after results from the clinic's own site). No stock, no generated photos.
- The owner approved: no offer section, before/after section, professional names in reviews may stay.

## Evidence on Hand
- Google Business Profile: 5,0, 391 reviews. Seven substantive 5-star reviews saved in data/google-reviews.json.
- Storefront photo, number 1422 visible: assets/source/google-maps/fachada-1422.jpg (1200x676).
- Six before/after composites (500x500, clinic watermark): assets/source/site-antigo/.
- Absent: e-mail, responsible dentist and CRO, team photos, interior photos of this unit, higher-resolution results.

## Product Principles
1. Proof before promise: every claim is backed by something a visitor can verify (Google link, real photos, real address).
2. Warmth over clinical coldness; many visitors fear the dentist. Say it plainly, never joke about pain.
3. One action everywhere: schedule an evaluation on WhatsApp, same label each time.
4. Nothing invented. Missing data is omitted and listed for the owner.
5. Phone first, fast and light.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, alt text, reduced motion, touch targets of at least 44px. The clinic's Google listing states it welcomes the LGBTQ+ community; the site does not make that a headline claim without the owner's wording.
